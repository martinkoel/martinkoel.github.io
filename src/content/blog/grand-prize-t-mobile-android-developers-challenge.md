## The challenge

T-Mobile is introducing Android in the Netherlands and has organised a challenge around it. I won this challenge today!

![Martin wins the T-Mobile Android contest](/img/blog/490androidwedstrijd.jpg)

## Meter Maid

Through the Google developers programme I have had an Android phone at my disposal for some time. With it I made an application that makes paid parking a lot easier.

Paid parking is normally a search for the right zone number, with which you then have to compose a cryptic SMS. With Meter Maid this is a thing of the past.

![Meter Maid screen](/img/blog/metermaidscreenshot.jpg) 

Through a simple and fast user interface you start and stop the parking meter. The jury considered it important that it is a truly "mobile" application.
