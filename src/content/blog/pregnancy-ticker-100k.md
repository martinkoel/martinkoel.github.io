Well, I never saw this coming. Originally made for following the progress on our baby, who now is 1 year old. After releasing it in the market many nice comments were added and requests for features were mailed. So I added some extra stuff and within 2 years it passed the 100.000 downloads barrier. It is very difficult to imagine 100.000 persons using Pregnancy Ticker – it means that the progress of thousands of pregnancies has been followed with it – unbelievable.

I am planning to add more features to celebrate this moment and am actually working on one right now. It is a bit of a technical challenge, but more about that later.

Thanks for all the nice comments and best wishes!
