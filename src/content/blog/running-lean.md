Today I had the chance to meet [Ash Maurya](http://leanstack.com/). The Dutch Lean Startup Circle and Utrecht had invited him to give a workshop for Dutch startups.  

Ash is the author of *Running Lean*. The way the book came about is itself a great example of applying his motto: *Get out and learn!*  

![_Running Lean](/img/blog/ash-maurya-utrecht.jpg)

## Practice what you preach

He only started writing the book after gauging interest through his blog. He only picked up the pen once a minimum number of interested people had signed up.  

It was also nice to practice using [Alex Osterwalder](http://alexosterwalder.com/)'s business model canvas in the workshop. By using this [canvas](http://businessmodelgeneration.com/), you can map out your entire business model in a very short time.
