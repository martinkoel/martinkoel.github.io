Rob Ewaschuk wrote an interesting booklet about his observations at Google: [My Philosophy on Alerting](https://docs.google.com/a/boxever.com/document/d/199PqyG3UsyXlwieHaqbGiWVa8eMWi8zzAn0YfcApr8Q/edit) . It is a recommended read!

A short summary: 
- keep alerting simple
- alert on symptoms (‘monitor for your users’)

The booklet formed the basis for writing [https://www.oreilly.com/ideas/monitoring-distributed-systems](https://www.oreilly.com/ideas/monitoring-distributed-systems)
