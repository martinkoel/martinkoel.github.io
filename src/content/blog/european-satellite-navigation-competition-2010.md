## Meter Maid at the ESNC

Together with Dialogues Technology, I took part in the [European Satellite Navigation Competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Satellite_Navigation_Competition) with my parking application "Meter Maid". 

![](/img/blog/20101029_02-600x399.jpg)

The ESNC is organised every year by the European space organisation ESA to find new applications for the upcoming competitor of GPS, namely "Galileo". Galileo is more accurate than GPS and therefore offers many new possibilities.

![](/img/blog/20101029_03.jpg)

## Making parking easier

By providing the Meter Maid app with information about parking zones and their geographical boundaries, ease of use increases. A working prototype (using GPS) already selects the right zone almost every time and makes it possible to stop and start parking actions automatically.

The organisation rewarded this application with a prize for its innovative character.

![](/img/blog/esnc_2010_winners_node_full_image_2.jpg)

## The demonstration

Together with Bastiaan, I gave a demonstration of the idea and the implementation. 

![](/img/blog/20101029_05-600x447.jpg)
